Privacy Policy for My Notes

Effective Date: November 2026

My Notes is designed with privacy first. All notes you create are stored locally on your device.

Google User Data

If you choose to sign in with your Google Account, the app requests access solely to the hidden Google Drive application data folder (drive.appdata) to store and restore your encrypted/JSON note backup file. We do not collect, read, transmit, or share your notes or personal Google account information with any external servers or third parties.
